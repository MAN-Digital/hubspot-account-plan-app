---
title: Credits and budgets
description: Output-based credit accounting and rep caps.
---

Credits are output-based.

## No debit

- HubSpot-source reads.
- Provider setup checks.
- Prospecting preview.
- Viewing existing CRM contacts, associations, campaigns, or signals.

## Debit

- Accepted usable prospecting contacts.
- Generated or regenerated outreach cadences.
- Saved account research output.
- Buying group generation or regeneration.

## Rep caps

Admins can configure daily, weekly, and monthly caps per rep. A debit is blocked when a rep cap or tenant balance would be exceeded.
