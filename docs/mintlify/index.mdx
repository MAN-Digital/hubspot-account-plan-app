HAP Signal Workspace runs inside the HubSpot company record and keeps HubSpot as the CRM source of truth.

The workspace is built around one operational rule: show a credible reason to act now, the real people attached to that account, the gaps that need better data, and the safest next outreach action.

## Core surfaces

- HubSpot `crm.record.tab` card for company records.
- HubSpot app settings page for provider keys, budgets, themes, signal rules, and outreach angles.
- Backend API hosted outside HubSpot and called by `hubspot.fetch()`.
- Postgres-backed tenant data with no silent CRM writes.

## Provider model

- HubSpot reads and associations are source-of-truth CRM context.
- Apollo powers structured People Search and accepted-contact enrichment.
- HarvestAPI contributes LinkedIn profile and post evidence for prospecting context.
- Trigify powers recurring observed signal monitoring.
- Woodpecker is the custom-copy campaign delivery option and is reuse-first by design.
